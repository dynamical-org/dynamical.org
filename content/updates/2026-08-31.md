## HRRR family reunion

The HRRR story is filled out: 5 products for analysis and forecasts, spatial- and time-optimized, _every_ model initialization and variable.

![How far did the June 2023 Canadian wildfire smoke reach?](./assets/hrrr-analysis.png)

The last one was a virtual HRRR analysis, complete with all variables and levels. Check them all out here: https://dynamical.org/catalog/#noaa-hrrr

## data.dynamical.org deprecations

Technically the website says:

> data.dynamical.org access ends August 31, 2026. If you use a data.dynamical.org URL for this dataset, migrate to a supported access pattern.

But we will extend the deadline to September 30.

We put together a very brief [migration guide](/migration-2026/).

Some of you continue to drive a pretty staggering amount of bandwidth to this route. So check your stuff! It's going away in favor of hittin' the STAC or using [dynamical-catalog](https://github.com/dynamical-org/dynamical-catalog) which is like, better in every way. The proxy was just slowing you down, y'know. We are going to deprecate gently and go data product by data product. Tell your friends, especially if they're the one slammin' the proxy every Thursday with their doom cron.

## Validation reports for all

If you're like us and have lost days running down why your model isn't training and realized it's because a data oddity back in 2017, these reports are for you/your agent. Also useful for anyone who wants to get a quick visual sense of any variable in the dataset. 

![one of the validation plots we use to spot check variable correctness](./assets/spatial_wind_gust_surface.png)

**Every dataset in our catalog now has a detailed validation report.** They are in our STAC too so you can pass `https://stac.dynamical.org/catalog.json` to your agent and save yourself hours.
