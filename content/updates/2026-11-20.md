## IFS ENS backfilled to mid-2020

![ECMWF IFS ENS archive before and after the backfill: start moves from 2024-04-01 to 2020-10-15](/assets/ifs-ens-backfill-timeline.png)

[ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 15 day, 0.25 degree](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-15-day-0-25-degree/) (materialized): The archive used to start 2024-04-01. **It now starts 2020-10-15.** That's 1,264 more days of forecasts. One trillion MARS tears were shed in the process.

## IFS ENS, virtual

Every variable ECMWF puts in the ensemble files (surface, four soil layers, pressure levels), all 51 members, optimized for map access and low latency. Four flavors: the 6 day runs 4 times a day at 3 hourly steps, and the 15 day runs twice a day and goes 6 hourly after day 6. Each comes at 0.25 degree and at 0.1 degree (!). ECMWF only started publishing the 0.1 degree files this October, so those archives are short.

- [ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 15 day, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-15-day-0-25-degree-virtual/): archive starts 2024-04-01.
- [ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 6 day, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-6-day-0-25-degree-virtual/): archive starts 2024-04-01.
- [ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 15 day, 0.1 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-15-day-0-1-degree-virtual/): archive starts 2026-10-21.
- [ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 6 day, 0.1 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-6-day-0-1-degree-virtual/): archive starts 2026-10-21.

We are fully aware that the virtuals are getting overwhelming and are thinking of how to group them and make the catalog more navigable. If you have any thoughts, let us know at feedback@dynamical.org

## AIFS ENS, virtual

[ECMWF AIFS ENS forecast, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-aifs-ens-forecast-virtual/): the ensemble version of ECMWF's AI model, and a sibling to the [AIFS Single virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-aifs-single-forecast-virtual/) from last time. 51 members, 0.25 degree, 15 days at 6 hourly steps, 4 times a day. Archive starts 2025-07-01.
