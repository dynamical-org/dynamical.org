Salutations. _Operation Overwhelmingly Virtual_ is underway, and we've tried to deluge our fans and enemies with Unforgettable Data Product Experiences (TM).

In the last few weeks we've released 3 materialized and 7 (!) virtual artisanal Icechunk Zarrs. We hope you like them.

## ECCC HRDPS forecast

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/eccc-hrdps-forecast.jpg" alt="ECCC HRDPS forecast" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

[ECCC HRDPS forecast](https://dynamical.org/catalog/eccc-hrdps-forecast/) (materialized): Canada's high resolution, 48-hour, deterministic forecast. Archive starts 2026-07-09 and we may be able to backfill further.

## ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 46 day

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-daily-1-5-degree.jpg" alt="ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 46 day, daily" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

101 dalmatians, er, ensemble members every day from ECMWF's IFS at the sub-seasonal horizon. Comes in [daily](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-daily-1-5-degree/) and [six hourly](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-6-hourly-1-5-degree/) flavors (both materialized). Our archive starts 2026-01-01 and pairs well with Planette's 10 member reforecast that runs through 2025 [TODO: verify the Planette reforecast's name, link, member count, and coverage through 2025]. ECMWF's license holds these forecasts back for 48 hours, so each one arrives about two days after its `init_time`.

## ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/ecmwf-aifs-single-forecast-virtual.jpg" alt="ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

[ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-aifs-single-forecast-virtual/): all variables and pressure levels, low latency, and optimized for map access. Archive starts 2024-04-01.

## NOAA GFS and GEFS, virtual

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/noaa-gfs-forecast-virtual.jpg" alt="NOAA GFS forecast, virtual" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

Six virtual products, all global, optimized for spatial (map) access, with every variable. Chunks reference NOAA's GRIB files in the AWS Open Data Registry.

- [NOAA GFS forecast, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gfs-forecast-virtual/): 0.25°, 0-16 days (hourly to 120 hours, then 3-hourly), initialized every 6 hours. Archive starts 2021-05-01.
- [NOAA GFS analysis, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gfs-analysis-virtual/): 0.25°, hourly. Archive starts 2021-05-01.
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 10 day, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-10-day-0-25-degree-virtual/): 31 members, 0.25°, 0-10 days at 3-hourly steps, initialized every 6 hours. Archive starts 2020-10-01.
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 16 day, 0.5 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-16-day-0-5-degree-virtual/): 31 members, 0.5°, 0-16 days (3-hourly to 240 hours, then 6-hourly), initialized every 6 hours. Archive starts 2020-10-01.
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 35 day, 0.5 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-35-day-0-5-degree-virtual/): 31 members, 0.5°, 0-35 days (3-hourly to 240 hours, then 6-hourly), 00 UTC initializations only. Archive starts 2020-10-01.
- [NOAA GEFS analysis, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-analysis-0-25-degree-virtual/): 0.25°, 3-hourly. Archive starts 2020-10-01.
