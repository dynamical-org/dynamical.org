Salutations. _Operation Overwhelmingly Virtual_ is underway, and we've tried to deluge our fans and enemies with Unforgettable Data Product Experiences (TM).

In the last few weeks we've released 3 materialized and 7 (!) virtual artisanal Icechunk Zarrs. We hope you like them.

## ECCC HRDPS forecast

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/eccc-hrdps-forecast.jpg" alt="ECCC HRDPS forecast" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

[ECCC HRDPS forecast](https://dynamical.org/catalog/eccc-hrdps-forecast/) (materialized): Canada's high resolution, 48-hour, deterministic forecast. Archive starts 2026-07-09 and we may be able to backfill further.

## ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 46 day

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-daily-1-5-degree.jpg" alt="ECMWF IFS ENS forecast, 46 day, daily" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

101 dalmatians, er, ensemble members every day from ECMWF's IFS at the sub-seasonal horizon. Comes in [daily](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-daily-1-5-degree/) and [six hourly](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-ifs-ens-forecast-46-day-6-hourly-1-5-degree/) flavors (both materialized). Our archive starts 2026-01-01 and pairs well with [Planette's 10 member reforecast](https://github.com/PlanetteAI/planette_ifs_archive) that runs through 2025. ECMWF's license holds these forecasts back for 48 hours, so each one arrives about two days after its `init_time`.

## ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/ecmwf-aifs-single-forecast-virtual.jpg" alt="ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

[ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/ecmwf-aifs-single-forecast-virtual/): all variables and pressure levels, low latency, and optimized for map access. Archive starts 2024-04-01.

## NOAA GFS and GEFS, virtual

<img src="/assets/catalog-thumbnails/noaa-gfs-forecast-virtual.jpg" alt="NOAA GFS forecast, virtual" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover;">

Do you ever go to pick up something gigantic and are surprised to find how light it is? That's what it feels like to use these GFS + GEFS virtuals which are both mind-bendingly large and as easy to use as the rest of our datasets. Six global products, optimized for spatial (map) access and low latency with every variable, vertical level, and initialization. Chunks reference NOAA Open Data Dissemination GRIB files in the AWS Open Data Registry.

- [NOAA GFS forecast, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gfs-forecast-virtual/)
- [NOAA GFS analysis, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gfs-analysis-virtual/)
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 10 day, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-10-day-0-25-degree-virtual/)
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 16 day, 0.5 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-16-day-0-5-degree-virtual/)
- [NOAA GEFS forecast, 35 day, 0.5 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-forecast-35-day-0-5-degree-virtual/)
- [NOAA GEFS analysis, 0.25 degree, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-gefs-analysis-0-25-degree-virtual/)
