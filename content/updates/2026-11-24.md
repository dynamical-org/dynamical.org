The ICO in ICON is for icosahedral: the triangular grid the model runs on. Now you can get ICON-EU forecasts on that grid, too.

## DWD ICON-EU, icosahedral

[DWD ICON-EU forecast, 5 day, icosahedral](https://dynamical.org/catalog/dwd-icon-eu-forecast-5-day-icosahedral/) is live and updating. It covers Europe at 6.5 km resolution, with five-day forecasts initialized every six hours. The archive starts 2026-10-07.

Keeping the native grid avoids oversampling onto latitude and longitude, making the dataset about 13% smaller. The spatial dimension is a single `cell` dimension, which can take some getting used to. We've included metadata to help common libraries work with it, and the [quickstart notebook](https://github.com/dynamical-org/notebooks/blob/main/dwd-icon-eu-forecast-5-day-icosahedral.ipynb) walks through spatial selection and regridding.

If you're using the existing [latitude/longitude ICON-EU dataset](https://dynamical.org/catalog/dwd-icon-eu-forecast-5-day/), it will stay available with its longer history, but **it stops updating on November 30, 2026**. Switch to the icosahedral dataset for fresh forecasts.

## NOAA RTMA, RTMA-RU, and URMA

Three NOAA near-surface analyses are now in the catalog. All cover the continental United States at 2.5 km resolution. These are virtual datasets, referencing NOAA's GRIB files and optimized for map access.

- [NOAA RTMA analysis, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-rtma-analysis-virtual/): hourly analyses, with an archive starting 2017-05-02.
- [NOAA RTMA-RU analysis, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-rtma-ru-analysis-virtual/): rapid updates every 15 minutes, with an archive starting 2017-12-13.
- [NOAA URMA analysis, virtual](https://dynamical.org/catalog/noaa-urma-analysis-virtual/): hourly analyses incorporating later-arriving observations, with an archive starting 2017-05-02.

RTMA combines observations with a model background. URMA trades speed for a more complete historical analysis and serves as the National Weather Service's analysis of record for forecast verification. Check the catalog pages for examples and the variable lists.
