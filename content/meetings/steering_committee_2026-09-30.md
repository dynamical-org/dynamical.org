## Dataset quality and usage

We reviewed the summer's work on virtual datasets and automated validation. Building on earlier committee suggestions, plots and statistics help check variables and make dataset quality easier to inspect. September was our largest month so far for data transferred.

The committee also heard how rechunked datasets were helping seasonal forecasting work in settings with limited bandwidth.

## Icechunk migration

We discussed the gradual retirement of the legacy Zarr access path, starting with smaller datasets. Migration guidance and newsletter notices help users move to the supported access patterns while we monitor the transition.

## Committee direction

Members discussed broadening the committee's work beyond technical questions to include governance, sustainability and relationships with data providers. The discussion emphasized the value of diverse user perspectives and stronger connections with the agencies producing the data. This was a proposal for the committee's evolution, rather than a finalized membership change.

## Sustaining open data

The committee explored ways to share the cost of maintaining open data infrastructure. Discussion distinguished support and additional services from collective cost recovery for expensive archives.

One idea was a cooperative funding model in which contributions per participant decrease as more users join. Members also suggested gathering pledges to assess demand before committing to a large archive. These were ideas for further exploration, not an announced service.
